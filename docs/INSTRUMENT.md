# Blank post-block instrument

The post-block instrument used in the study is available in its original blank Google Form:

<https://forms.gle/61X7Wju3bVjaPJxy6>

It contains:

1. identification fields present in the original collection form;
2. the 10 System Usability Scale (SUS) items, answered on a five-point scale;
3. the eight UEQ-S semantic differentials, answered on a seven-point scale; and
4. four open-ended questions about navigation facilitators and difficulties, the role of the home screen, and possible changes to its organization.

For a study replication, do not collect direct identifiers unless they are necessary, approved by the applicable ethics process, and kept separate from model-facing context and public research outputs.
